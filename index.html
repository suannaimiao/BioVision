Concept Overview

Water is essential for life due to its unique chemical properties. These properties include cohesion ×

Cohesion

The attraction between molecules of the same substance. In water, hydrogen bonds cause molecules to stick together.

, adhesion ×
Adhesion

The attraction between molecules of different substances. Water adheres to surfaces, allowing capillary action.

 , high specific heat, and being a universal solvent. These characteristics allow water to support cellular functions and maintain homeostasis.

Key Points:

  • Water molecules form hydrogen bonds
  • High heat capacity stabilizes temperatures
  • Excellent solvent for polar molecules
  • Ice floats due to lower density

Why is water considered a polar molecule?

Added: 2023-10-15

Your Answer: Because it has no charge

Correct Answer: Because it has uneven charge distribution with oxygen being slightly negative and hydrogen slightly positive

Explanation: Water molecules are polar due to the electronegativity difference between oxygen and hydrogen atoms, creating partial charges.

Unit 1 Water Properties Polarity Medium

DNA Replication Fork Diagram Analysis

Added: 2023-10-10

Your Answer: Leading strand synthesis is discontinuous

Correct Answer: Leading strand synthesis is continuous, while lagging strand is discontinuous (Okazaki fragments)

Explanation: Due to the anti-parallel nature of DNA and the 5'→3' synthesis direction, the leading strand is synthesized continuously while the lagging strand is synthesized in fragments.

Unit 4 DNA Replication Molecular Biology Hard

Enzyme Kinetics Calculation

Added: 2023-10-05

Your Answer: Vmax = 50 μmol/min, Km = 2 mM

Correct Answer: Vmax = 100 μmol/min, Km = 1 mM

Explanation: From the Lineweaver-Burk plot, intercept = 1/Vmax, slope = Km/Vmax. Recalculate using proper linear regression.
