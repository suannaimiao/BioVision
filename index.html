Properties of Water and Life

Unit 1 / Chapter 1 / Concept 1

Personified Animation (5 min)

Watch an engaging animated story that personifies water molecules and their interactions in living systems.

Concept Overview

Water is essential for life due to its unique chemical properties. These properties include cohesion ×

Cohesion

The attraction between molecules of the same substance. In water, hydrogen bonds cause molecules to stick together.

, adhesion ×
Adhesion

The attraction between molecules of different substances. Water adheres to surfaces, allowing capillary action.

, high specific heat, and being a universal solvent. These characteristics allow water to support cellular functions and maintain homeostasis.

Key Points:

  • Water molecules form hydrogen bonds
  • High heat capacity stabilizes temperatures
  • Excellent solvent for polar molecules
  • Ice floats due to lower density
