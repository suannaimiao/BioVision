Properties of Water and Life

Unit 1 / Chapter 1 / Concept 1

Video: Properties of Water (15:32)

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the unique properties of water that make it essential for life
  • Explain hydrogen bonding and its role in water's properties
  • Describe cohesion, adhesion, and surface tension
  • Understand water's role as a universal solvent

Key Concepts

Polarity

Water molecules have a slight positive charge on the hydrogen atoms and a slight negative charge on the oxygen atom

Hydrogen Bonding

Weak bonds between water molecules that give water its unique properties

Cohesion

Water molecules sticking to other water molecules

Adhesion

Water molecules sticking to other substances
